# Intro

I grew up in Russia, studied in Moscow and Toulouse, and now work in Frankfurt — so moving between languages and contexts is fairly normal for me at this point. I speak Russian, English, French, and German, and I hold a Mathematics Master's from Toulouse School of Economics and a degree from Higher School of Economics, where I also spent a few years teaching data science and macroeconomics on the side.

My background is a bit of a mix: I started out managing operations in a small agricultural biotech startup, moved into credit risk analytics and statistical modelling at Tinkoff Bank, spent time on macroeconomics and ML research, and eventually found my way into quantitative and AI research for financial markets — which is what I am focusing on now.

Right now I'm part of the 2025–2026 graduate cohort at BNP Paribas, working in the Data&AI Lab at Global Markets in Frankfurt. The work sits somewhere between trading research and AI engineering — from ML-based signals and market microstructure to agentic systems. The thing I find most interesting is the space where rigorous quantitative models meet modern AI: not replacing one with the other, but figuring out how they fit together.
